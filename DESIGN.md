# DESIGN.md — Manish Bhat Portfolio

## Concept Card
Idea:        Dual-Signal Identity: precision AI Engineer by day, interactive Alter-Ego ("Spider-Sense") mode on hover/toggle.
Metaphor:    A live telemetry & field-manual dossier that flips from Polaris Obsidian to Spider-Sense Crimson across one global variable.
Signature:   The Alter-Ego Switch (portrait hover/click, header pill, or `S` key) paired with an interactive Project Index + cursor-following preview and iris page transitions.
Emotion:     Sharp, confident engineering craft with an unmistakable playful streak underneath.
Motion verb: Snap and lock (crisp expo.out entrances, taut pointer-fill buttons, clean iris curtain).
Screenshot:  Hero frame with oversized Space Grotesk display type, live coordinate strip, portrait frame, and the Alter-Ego toggle.

## Art Direction
- Ground (Default / Polaris): #090B0E | Surface: #11151C | Ink: #F4F5F7 | Muted: #94A3B8 | Line: rgba(244,245,247,0.12) | Accent: #22D3EE
- Ground (Alter-Ego / Spidey): #0D0709 | Surface: #170C10 | Ink: #F8F2F3 | Muted: #FDA4AF | Line: rgba(248,242,243,0.14) | Accent: #F43F5E
- Display & Body: Space Grotesk (self-hosted woff2) | Data & Labels: JetBrains Mono (self-hosted woff2)
- Radius: 2px sharp technical corners (portrait frame: 4px)
